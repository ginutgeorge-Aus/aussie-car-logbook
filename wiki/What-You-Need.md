# What You Need

Setting up your own copy is a **one-time** job. Here's everything to have
ready before you start the [Setup Guide](Setup-Guide).

## The short list

| # | What | Why | Cost |
|---|------|-----|------|
| 1 | A **free Cloudflare account** | This is where your app and data live | Free |
| 2 | A **Google account or an email address** | To lock the app so only you can open it | Free |
| 3 | A **computer** (Windows, Mac, or Linux) | To run the one-time setup commands | — |
| 4 | About **30 minutes** | For the first-time setup | — |
| 5 | Your **car details** | Make, model, rego, odometer reading | — |

That's it. No credit card, no monthly fee.

## A little about each

**Cloudflare account** — Cloudflare is the company that hosts the app for
free. You'll create an account at [cloudflare.com](https://cloudflare.com) if
you don't have one. Your data (trips, receipts) is stored inside *your*
Cloudflare account — not shared with anyone, including the people who wrote
this app.

**Google account or email** — the app has no username/password of its own.
Instead it uses a Cloudflare feature called **Access** to make sure only *you*
can open it. You tell it "only let in this Google account" (or "only this
email, via a one-time code"). See the [Setup Guide](Setup-Guide).

**A computer for setup** — the first-time setup uses a few typed commands.
You don't need to be a programmer; the [Setup Guide](Setup-Guide) gives you
the exact commands to copy and paste. After setup, you use the app on your
**phone** and never touch the computer again (unless you want to update it).

## Do I need to know how to code?

**No.** You'll copy and paste some commands during setup, but you don't need
to understand or write any code. If you can follow a recipe, you can do this.

If even that feels like too much, ask a tech-comfortable friend to do the
30-minute [Setup Guide](Setup-Guide) once — after that, everything happens on
your phone.
