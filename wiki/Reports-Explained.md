# Reports Explained

The **Reports** page turns your trips and expenses into the numbers you
actually need: your **BAS GST credit** and your **annual tax deduction**.

Open Reports, pick a **financial year** from the dropdown, and you'll see the
sections below. (Australian financial year = **1 July to 30 June**.)

> ⚠️ These are estimates for record-keeping. The app itself says: *"Confirm
> figures with your accountant."* Not tax advice.

---

## At the top: business-use %

The page shows your **business-use %** for the chosen year (from your trips —
see [Understanding the Logbook Method](Understanding-the-Logbook-Method)).
This percentage drives every dollar figure below it.

---

## BAS — GST credit by quarter

If you're **GST-registered**, you can claim back the business share of the GST
you paid on car costs, through your **Business Activity Statement (BAS)**.

The report shows a table:

| Quarter | Covers | GST credit |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Q1 | Jul – Sep | $… |
| Q2 | Oct – Dec | $… |
| Q3 | Jan – Mar | $… |
| Q4 | Apr – Jun | $… |
| **Total** | full year | **$…** |

**How it's worked out:** for each quarter, the app adds up the GST on your
receipts, then multiplies by your business-use %.

> GST credit ≈ business-use % × (total GST paid that quarter)

So when you lodge each quarterly BAS, read the GST credit off the matching
row.

> 💡 Not GST-registered? You can ignore the BAS section — you don't claim GST
> credits, and instead your deduction is based on the full (GST-inclusive)
> amounts.

---

## Annual income-tax deduction

This is the figure for your **tax return**. It has two parts:

| Line | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| **Running costs (business share)** | Your business-use % of fuel, rego, insurance, servicing, repairs, tyres, interest/lease, etc. |
| **Car depreciation (business share)** | Your business-use % of the car's decline in value (only if you entered purchase date + cost) |
| **Total deduction** | The two added together — the number you claim |

> **GST-registered note:** because you already claim the GST back through your
> BAS, the income-tax deduction uses the **GST-exclusive** amounts, so you
> don't double-dip.

### Expenses by category

Below the totals, there's a breakdown of your expenses by category (fuel,
rego, insurance, …) at the GST-inclusive amount — handy for checking nothing's
missing or miscategorised.

---

## Notices (amber box)

Sometimes an amber note appears, for example that **depreciation was left out**
because your car's purchase-year cost limit isn't configured yet. This is a
one-line fix in the app — see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting). Your other
figures are still correct.

---

## Print / save it

The **Print** button produces a clean copy (navigation stripped out) — print
it or "save as PDF". Do this:

- **Each BAS quarter** — for your GST credit figure.
- **At tax time** — for your deduction, to keep or give to your accountant.

---

## Quick recap

- **Business-use %** — from your trips.
- **BAS section** — quarterly GST credits (GST-registered only).
- **Annual deduction** — running costs + depreciation, your business share.
- **Print** — keep the record.
