# Ginoo's Log Book

**A free car logbook for Australian tax — on your phone, owned by you.**

Ginoo's Log Book helps you claim your work-related car expenses the way the
ATO expects. You log your trips and your car costs (fuel, servicing, rego,
insurance), keep photos of your receipts, and the app works out how much of
your car use is for work — then produces the numbers you need for your BAS
and your tax return.

It runs as an app on your phone (a "PWA" — you add it to your home screen,
no app store needed) and all your data stays in **your own** private copy.
Nobody else can see your trips or receipts.

> ⚠️ **Important:** This is not tax advice. It does the sums for you using the
> ATO **logbook method**, but you are responsible for what you claim. If in
> doubt, check with a registered tax agent.

## Is this for me?

**Yes, if you:**
- Drive your own car for work in Australia, and
- Want to claim car expenses on your tax return, and
- Are happy to keep a 12-week logbook of your trips.

**Maybe not, if you:**
- Use the cents-per-kilometre method only (this app is built for the
  **logbook method**), or
- Want a sign-up-and-go app with nothing to set up — this one you host
  yourself (it's free, but there's a one-time setup).
